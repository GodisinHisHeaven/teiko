Two-sided Mann–Whitney U test with ties and continuity correction. Holm correction covers five populations. CIs are unadjusted bootstrap intervals for the mean difference.
Methods and interpretation

Repeated visits

Each subject contributes their average sample percentage across the selected visits. The boxplots can also show individual samples, but statistical tests always use subjects as independent observations.

Effect and uncertainty

Mean differences use percentage points. The 95% percentile intervals resample subjects separately within each response group 2,000 times with a fixed seed. Full exports include medians, U statistics, sample sizes and Cliff’s delta.

What the comparison establishes

These are exploratory response associations. Post-treatment observations cannot establish a baseline predictor. The baseline view is more relevant to prediction, but still needs a held-out validation cohort. Project, age and sex are not adjusted for.

Five parts of a whole

Frequencies sum to 100% over the five measured populations. A relative increase may reflect changes in other populations. Holm correction controls the familywise error rate without requiring independent tests.

References: SciPy Mann–Whitney U · Holm (1979)

ADDITIONAL BASELINE QUESTION

Average B-cell count in male responders

View the SQL queries

The counts below use distinct subjects for response and sex. The additional B-cell question intentionally includes all treatments and sample types.

SELECT * FROM baseline_miraclib_melanoma_pbmc;

SELECT project, COUNT(*) AS samples
FROM baseline_miraclib_melanoma_pbmc GROUP BY project;

SELECT response, COUNT(DISTINCT subject) AS subjects
FROM baseline_miraclib_melanoma_pbmc GROUP BY response;

SELECT sex, COUNT(DISTINCT subject) AS subjects
FROM baseline_miraclib_melanoma_pbmc GROUP BY sex;

SELECT AVG(c.count)
FROM sample_metadata AS m JOIN cell_counts AS c USING (sample)
WHERE m.condition = 'melanoma' AND m.sex = 'M'
  AND m.response = 'yes' AND m.time_from_treatment_start = 0
  AND c.population = 'b_cell';
